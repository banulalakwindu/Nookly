A little notch
that does a lot

Nookly sits quietly at the top of your Windows screen. Hover it for music with lyrics, your calendar and next meeting, notes, countdowns, timers, health breaks, quick toggles and more. Move away and it tucks itself back. Want more? Add Nookly Taskbar, a whole new taskbar with live widgets.

Light on your PC

Tiny download, tiny footprint

Nookly is one small program written in Rust and drawn on the GPU. It wakes for what happens and sleeps the rest of the time, so you can leave it running all day.

~3 MBThe whole download. No runtime, framework or browser engine to install alongside it.
~30 MBOf memory in everyday use, opening it and switching panels. Less than a browser tab.
~0% CPUAt rest. A song changing, a hover or a window moving wakes it; nothing polls.
Off is offA feature you switch off doesn't run at all: no work, no memory.
Optional add-on

Nookly Taskbar

A whole new Windows taskbar in the notch's bubble shape and theme. It isn't in the Nookly download: get it only if you want it, and without it Nookly stays exactly as light.

  • Your apps with live previews, a peek at any window, recent files, progress and badges.
  • 32 widgets in up to four sizes, each opening into its own view.
  • On every screen, with its own choices for the other ones.
  • Under 1 MB, and Windows' taskbar always comes back.

Only what you need

Switch on just the parts you use

All in Settings, now in its own window: a master switch for the notch, then start from a preset and turn anything on or off. A feature that's off doesn't run at all, so Nookly stays light, written in Rust and drawn on the GPU.

MinimalStudentWorkEverything
  • Win+Alt+N opens the notch from anywhere; set your own keys for clipboard and screenshots.
  • Too many panels? The extras tuck under ⋯, so the tab bar never gets crowded.
  • New PC? Export your settings to one file and import them there.
  • Your order. Drag the panels into the order you like; set your location once for the weather.
  • Want a taskbar too? Nookly Taskbar is an optional add-on with its own switch in Settings; without it, nothing of it runs.
Everything in one notch

Small on the outside, a lot inside

Each panel is one hover away. Roll the mouse wheel on the open notch to move between them.

Music and synced lyrics

Any media app: Spotify, YouTube in the browser, Apple Music. Controls, a seek bar and lyrics that light up line by line.

Widgets on the notch

Battery, AirPods, weather, Wi-Fi, CPU and more beside the clock. You pick what shows and in which order.

Your notifications

Messages, mail and calendar alerts drop out of the notch with the app's icon, instead of the corner of the screen.

AI agents

Claude Code, Codex and Hermes: see when they're working, when one needs you, and when it's done.

Calendar and meetings

Link your Google or Outlook calendar: "Standup in 8 min" on the notch, and Join for Teams, Zoom and Meet.

Weather

Now, hour by hour and the week ahead for your place, with icons for every kind of weather.

Volume mixer

The master volume and each app's own, with mute, one hover away.

Health

Screen time today, eye breaks, stand-up reminders and battery care. Reminders wait while you're in a game or on a call.

Countdowns

Exams, trips and birthdays, with "12 days" right on the closed notch.

Notes

A scratchpad that saves as you type, a shortcut away.

Launcher

Your apps, folders and favourite sites, one click away.

Quick toggles

Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, dark mode and microphone mute without digging through Settings.

Commands

Run your own commands on a schedule and see what they print on the notch.

Settings, in its own window

Every setting in one place, grouped, with a switch to turn the notch on or off, and a Taskbar tab for the optional taskbar add-on.

Liquid Glass

Real glass with no outline: the edges bend what's behind like a lens, and light drifts through it like water.

Ten themes

Black, Rose, Forest, Ocean, Paper, Liquid Glass and more, every one checked for readable contrast.

Timer and Pomodoro

Countdowns and focus sessions with the time left on the closed notch, so you never need another window.

To-do with reminders

Quick tasks with due times; the notch reminds you and lets you snooze or tick it off.

Clipboard history

Search what you copied, keep snippets, click to paste. Passwords from password managers are never kept.

File shelf and screenshots

Drop files on the notch to keep them close, then drag them into a chat. Screenshots land here too.

Handy tools

Colour picker, calculator, QR codes, unit converter with today's currency rates, and world clocks.

Prayer times and water

Prayer times calculated on your PC with gentle reminders, and a water reminder that counts your glasses.

Private by design

Your data stays on your PC

No account, no analytics, no ads. Everything Nookly keeps is plain text in your own AppData folder.

  • Offline first. It goes online only for lyrics, the weather widget if you switch it on, and checking for updates.
  • Opt-in. Clipboard history and Windows notifications stay off until you turn them on.
  • Open source. Read every line on GitHub; releases are built from it on GitHub's servers.
