Nookly and your privacy

Nookly runs on your PC and keeps your data there, in plain text files in
%APPDATA%\Nookly. It has no account, no telemetry, no ads and no
tracking.

It goes online for only these things, and sends no personal information:

  - Lyrics (LRCLIB, lrclib.net): only while you have the lyrics view open,
    it sends the title, artist, album and length of the song that's playing
    so it can find matching lyrics.

  - Updates (GitHub): about twice a day it asks GitHub for the latest version
    number. You can turn this off on the next page, or later in
    Settings > General > Check for updates automatically.

  - Weather (Open-Meteo, open-meteo.com): only if you switch the Weather
    widget on, about every 30 minutes, for your place rounded to about
    10 km; and the city name you type if you search for your city.

  - Your calendar: only if you paste its private iCal link in the Calendar
    tab, about every 15 minutes, straight from Google or Microsoft.

  - Currency rates (open.er-api.com): only after you ask for them in the
    Converter, at most once a day.

Mirror uses the camera only while its panel is open; nothing is saved.

Windows notifications in the notch and clipboard history are off until you
turn them on, and are read on your PC only.
